This month, we’re spotlighting Alice, a Member Advocate on our Family team with over a decade of experience in case management and patient advocacy. Before joining Cofertility, she led clinical operations at the Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia, supporting children with a genetic condition. Drawn to Cofertility’s human-centered mission, Alice brings warmth, empathy, and a steady hand to every member interaction.
